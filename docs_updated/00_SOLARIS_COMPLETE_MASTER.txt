SOLARIS 2.0 — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)
Version 3.0 | 31 October 2026 | Status: proposed hackathon implementation
Audience: founders, designers, mentors, product owners, OpenCode
Traceability: PRD-Fxx -> SRS-FR-xxx -> TRD module/API -> tests in Implementation Plan

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Solaris is an organisation-based solar decision and management platform with three authorised workspaces: (1) Households & Societies (retain the existing app), (2) Malls & Large Businesses (new B2B), and (3) Solar Installers (new supplier portal). The website includes Helios, a fun, accurate, permission-aware Gemini assistant. SolarMatch recommends suitable providers for BOTH households and commercial properties based on consumption, usable area, geography, financing constraints, policy eligibility and installer capabilities.

2. THE PROBLEMS
P1 Residential: neighbours have different budgets/usage; financing and participation require coordination; proposed credit allocations, actual bills and ownership must be distinguished; member exits raise transfer and approval questions.
P2 Commercial: facility teams struggle to compare quotes and system sizes; monthly bill totals hide demand charges and daytime load; tenants lack understandable energy-benefit statements; procurement, monitoring and data are fragmented.
P3 Installers: incoming leads often lack roof, demand, location and budget data; quotes and project milestones lack a standard exchange format.
P4 Across all three: people cannot easily understand bill calculations, VNM limits, assumptions and product decisions.

3. PRODUCT VISION AND VALUE
Tagline: 'Solaris — Plan it. Power it. Understand it.'
Residential: feasible community participation + transparent settlement + SmartExit.
Commercial: model before/after electricity costs + choose systems/installers + visually manage permitted tenant financial benefits.
Installer: receive matched enquiries, prepare structured quotations, track assigned projects.
Helios: answer general and context-specific questions without fabricating monetary values or bypassing RBAC.
Business hypothesis: property/society SaaS subscriptions and installer SaaS/project opportunities. Prices and demand unvalidated.

4. PERSONAS / RBAC
- Residential resident: own profile, own bills/budget, own proposed plans, approved credits, SmartExit requests.
- Society coordinator: own community/project membership, readiness, plans, approvals and aggregate outcomes; private files only when authorised.
- Mall/property operator: own commercial property, project-level bills, selected tenant benefit policy, procurement, high-level analytics.
- Facility/energy manager: designated property meter and operational data, uploads and technical reporting.
- Shop tenant: own shop consumption and statements; cannot see competing tenants' private information.
- Installer company admin: own company profile, leads explicitly shared with company, assigned quotes/projects.
- Installer engineer: technical project tasks assigned; cannot see resident financial ceilings or unrelated mall bills.
- Platform admin: system configuration/access audit, no routine private bill inspection.
One user may belong to multiple organisations with DIFFERENT roles. Only authorised workspaces appear in selector.

5. HACKATHON REFERENCE MODEL AND CRITICAL BOUNDARIES
The flagship commercial demo uses a SYNTHETIC 20–30-shop mall, one main DISCOM meter, shop sub-meters and a mall-owned rooftop plant. Actual energy enters the property's electrical network; sub-meters record shop consumption. Solaris calculates a simulated, contractually defined INTERNAL tenant benefit, NOT official shop-level VNM credits or a right to resell electricity. Official VNM is an optional mode ONLY for specifically eligible, approved connections with verifiable utility settlement. A contractual clause does not override law or existing leases. All numbers, approvals, tariff assumptions and providers in demo must be labelled synthetic.

6. MVP PRIORITIES
P0 mandatory: PRD-F01 workspace selection and multi-organisation RBAC; F02 preserve residential app; F03 commercial property/meter setup; F04 before/after commercial bill simulator with tariff assumptions; F05 SolarMatch for commercial and residential; F06 installer registration + quote handoff; F07 3D/2D mall map backed by shop records; F08 tenant statement with unambiguous benefit type; F09 Helios with safe role-scoped read tools; F10 demo seed and tests.
P1: reviewed bill OCR, actual-vs-projected savings, CSV generation/meter imports, residential SmartExit improvements, exportable reports.
P2/post-hack: live meter/DISCOM APIs, commercial VNM settlement, precise real-site 3D digital twins, learning-to-rank trained on verified outcomes, automated legal contracting/payment collection.

7. FUNCTIONAL FEATURES
PRD-F01 — Workspace selector. Three visual entry cards: Households & Societies, Malls & Businesses, Solar Installers. After login show membership-scoped workspaces. Never equate toggle selection with permission elevation.
PRD-F02 — Residential continuity. Keep current registration, VNM readiness, bill intake, feasibility, affordability/benefit-aware plan comparisons, consent and approval records, provider records, household financial view, credit reconciliation, actual-vs-projected tracking and SmartExit. Existing project data and routes must survive migrations.
PRD-F03 — Commercial onboarding. Property operator creates property, selects jurisdiction/DISCOM and connection arrangement (single-point, separate tenant connections, common-area), maps main/sub-meters, adds synthetic shops and roof area. Show meter diagram and eligibility warning.
PRD-F04 — Commercial Bill Intelligence. User imports bills and confirms tariff/charge lines; compares candidate PV capacities; receives before/after bills by charge component, annual savings, assumptions, uncertainty and approximate payback/NPV. Do not imply fixed or maximum demand charges vanish automatically.
PRD-F05 — SolarMatch. For homes, housing societies and malls, recommend feasible installation capacity range and filter installers by location/service, system size, declared experience, available budget, permissions/policy prerequisites. Rank suitable installers with transparent configurable weighted scoring. Compare quotes and show reason for no match. No fabricated verified badges.
PRD-F06 — Installer Portal. Installer organisation onboarding, company service area, certifications with evidence status, capacity range, structured quote and project milestones; leads appear only after customer shares them.
PRD-F07 — Visual Mall. Accessible interactive generic 3D mall, floor selector, shop cards; green=active arrangement, amber=pending, blue=proposed eligible, grey=none/unknown, red=exception; labels must distinguish 'Internal benefit' from 'Official VNM'. 2D fallback.
PRD-F08 — Tenant Benefit Ledger. Versioned policy defines permitted eligible savings, tenant participants, allocation formula, caps, rounding, and effective period. Statement shows measured usage, internal estimated solar attribution if applicable, economic adjustment, policy version, assumptions, and uncertainty. NEVER silently issue an official DISCOM bill.
PRD-F09 — Helios. Friendly, lightly humorous assistant ('Let me shed some light ☀️') available on each workspace; handles product FAQ, general solar education, tariff terms, user-specific own statements, project status, policy explanation, quote comparisons and SolarMatch via authorised read-only tools. It refuses to invent data or disclose other users' records.
PRD-F10 — Evidence and provenance. Identify demo, uploaded actual, manually corrected, estimated, officially approved, and unavailable data. Never show '100% reconciled' merely because shares add to 100%.
PRD-F11 — Residential SmartExit. Diagnose whether exit requires ownership transfer, outstanding finance treatment or approved credit-allocation update; compare willing replacement buyers / transfer splits with OR-Tools if constraints warrant; retain agreement and DISCOM approval gating. An exit alone does not imply money shortage.

8. VISUAL DESIGN / MINIMAL TEXT
- Dashboard information hierarchy: 3–4 KPI cards -> mall 3D model -> before/after bar chart -> energy split -> quick actions.
- On click shop: concise consumption/benefit/status/statement card, 'How calculated?' explanation.
- Helios floating sun bubble; suggestion chips such as 'Why is my bill still high?', 'Which installer suits my roof?', 'How is my benefit calculated?'.
- Text must be concise, with tooltips/expanders for detailed rules; colour never sole status indicator.
- Responsive, accessible low-motion fallback; no heavy 3D blocking first paint.

9. FIVE-STEP FLAGSHIP USER JOURNEY
Mall owner signs in -> creates synthetic mall + meters -> reviews uploaded/mock bills -> compares 100/200/300 kWp and before/after costs -> runs SolarMatch and sends quote request -> installer responds from its own workspace -> owner sees 3D shop benefits -> tenant opens own statement -> Helios explains inputs and differences. Briefly switch to existing residential workspace and show SmartExit and household SolarMatch.

10. ACCEPTANCE / SUCCESS
- Residential flows regressions: none; independent workspaces route correctly.
- Every shop panel maps to a scoped tenant and a recorded meter/benefit status.
- Recalculate same bill from stored input + versioned tariff yields same numeric result.
- SolarMatch hard filters always pass and scores expose factors.
- Installer sees only explicitly shared leads; tenant A cannot fetch tenant B's statement, including through Helios.
- Simulated generation/export is never described as official DISCOM credit.
- Demo end-to-end completes without relying on live external utility feeds.

11. EXCLUSIONS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
Out of scope: official utility billing, authorising VNM changes, electricity resale, automatic meter-to-shop power routing, contract enforcement, verified subsidy claims without sourced policy, production integrations. Validate local tariff/connection structure and whether tenant benefit sharing is allowed before a real pilot. Seek actual anonymised mall main bill, sub-meter statement, tenant agreement and site rooftop data.

SOLARIS 2.0 — SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (SRS)
Version 1.0 | 31 October 2026 | Status: design baseline, subject to repository audit
Requirement vocabulary: MUST = required hackathon behavior; SHOULD = desirable; MAY = future.

1. SCOPE, ACTORS, SYSTEM CONTEXT
Solaris is a browser-based multi-tenant platform for residential societies, commercial properties and installers. External services: identity provider, Gemini API (Helios), private file storage, optional solar/resource lookup. DISCOM and inverter are OFFLINE/mock/CSV in hackathon; never simulate a real approval silently.
Actors: anonymous visitor, authenticated resident, society admin, mall operator, facility manager, tenant, installer admin, installer engineer, platform admin. One user can have multiple membership/roles. All data reads and mutations are project/organisation scoped.

3. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
SRS-NFR-001 Security: TLS in deployment; passwords/tokens via established auth; API keys only backend; per-resource authorization mandatory; files private and signed short-lived links.
SRS-NFR-002 Privacy: minimum data exposed; no cross-tenant leakage; do not send whole bill archives to Gemini unnecessarily; configurable data retention/deletion policy.
SRS-NFR-003 Reliability: no silent errors; async OCR/AI may fail while deterministic financial tool remains operational; store calculation versions.
SRS-NFR-004 Performance goals for seeded hackathon load, not verified SLAs: page shell <3 seconds on typical laptop; shop details <1 second from local API; bill comparison <5 seconds for monthly profiles; 3D fallback under degraded hardware. Measure and report actual performance.
SRS-NFR-005 Accuracy: currency in integer paise or decimal fixed precision, not floating-point money; monetary results traceable to inputs and formulas; unit tests for rounding.
SRS-NFR-006 Accessibility: keyboard access to all important actions; color plus text label; semantic controls; reduced-motion option and 2D mall mode.
SRS-NFR-007 Scalability: one schema per platform with organisation_id partitioning/logical tenant isolation; indexed queries; paginated lists; optional caching; async workloads.
SRS-NFR-008 Observability: structured request logs with IDs and redaction; audit logs; monitor API errors and Gemini token/service failures without logging user secrets.
SRS-NFR-009 Maintainability: backend contract-first design, typed frontend clients, migration versioning, CI unit and integration suites.
SRS-NFR-010 Regulatory clarity: show state-specific effective tariff date and unknown approvals; no universal legality claims.

4. BUSINESS RULES / INVARIANTS
BR-01 Official VNM credit != internal tenant financial adjustment != projection.
BR-02 PV generation != on-site consumption != export != credited energy.
BR-03 Ownership contribution != proposed credit share != DISCOM-approved allocation.
BR-04 Only explicit consent/contract/policy permits internal financial adjustment; platform is not a utility.
BR-05 Main-meter and shop sub-meter readings cannot be naively summed as independent purchases from DISCOM.
BR-06 Fixed and demand charges do not disappear automatically on solar commissioning.
BR-07 In single-point mall mode, shops physically use the connected electrical network; per-shop 'solar kWh' is an accounting estimate unless interval/submeter evidence justifies a stronger claim.
BR-08 Installer match ranking must respect hard filters regardless of weighted score.
BR-09 Helios can explain but cannot change approved allocations, sign contracts, collect payment or approve credit.
BR-10 No exit automatically imposes cost on the remaining members; inspect legal obligations first.

5. DATA VALIDATION AND ERRORS
Reject negative kWh, invalid tariff dates, impossible share sum, missing meter associations, disallowed role combinations, duplicate invoice imports, mismatched periods and malformed quotes. Handle unknown billing rules with 'simulation unavailable until configuration is reviewed'. Show actionable validation UI. Prevent duplicate processing through idempotency keys.

SOLARIS 2.0 — TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS / DESIGN DOCUMENT (TRD)
Version 1.0 | 31 October 2026 | Status: reference architecture for OpenCode
Technology choices are proposals; inspect existing repository and reuse current libraries/host before migrating.

1. ARCHITECTURAL DECISION RECORDS
ADR-01 One multi-tenant platform, multiple workspaces. Rationale: reuse identity, bills, solar calculations, marketplace and AI; lower maintenance. Tradeoff: rigorous org-scoped authorization mandatory.
ADR-02 Mall hackathon uses MAIN_DISCOM + SUBMETER + INTERNAL_ADJUSTMENT; VNM only in approved-specific mode. Rationale: practical without live DISCOM dependency.
ADR-03 Deterministic rules for billing/allocation; Gemini only natural-language interaction and explanation. Rationale: auditability.
ADR-04 Scenario sweep for initial system sizes, OR-Tools CP-SAT for constrained allocations/SmartExit where needed. Rationale: avoid gratuitous optimisation.
ADR-05 Data-driven 3D map with accessible 2D fallback. Rationale: avoid UI slowing down or masking billing logic.
ADR-06 No automatic legal contract, payment collection or official credit distribution in hackathon.

2. REFERENCE DEPLOYMENT
Client: existing React/TypeScript application (if confirmed), Tailwind/shadcn/ui, Recharts or ECharts, React Three Fiber + Three.js for model.
API: Python FastAPI/Pydantic or adapt existing backend if already deployed. Modular route/service/repository architecture. Asynchronous task runner if bill OCR/report generation warrants it.
Storage: PostgreSQL, migrations, row-level security where appropriate, encrypted/signed private file bucket.
AI: Gemini API only through backend adapter; versioned prompts; safe tool schema and data minimisation.
Optimization: Google OR-Tools CP-SAT behind service; no solver needed for arithmetic credit reconciliation.
Financial computation: Decimal or integer paise and documented rounding.

6. COMMERCIAL BILL ENGINE MATHEMATICS
For interval t (prefer 15/30/60-min profiles): solar_self_use_t = min(load_t, pv_t); import_t = max(load_t-pv_t,0); export_t = max(pv_t-load_t,0).
E_before = tariff_energy(load_by_time); E_after = tariff_energy(import_by_time).
B_before = E_before + demand_before + fixed_before + surcharge_before + tax_before.
B_after = E_after + demand_after + fixed_after + surcharge_after + tax_after - eligible_export_credit.
Savings = B_before - B_after. Do not assume demand_before == demand_after unless justified; do not deduct solar credit twice. Document tariff assumptions, seasonality, profile approximation, connection constraints and export settlement. For monthly-only data, estimate self-use using transparent assumed profile or fraction and label ESTIMATED, never physically measured.
Use Decimal or paise, and explicit rounding. Simulations persist immutable snapshots so results can be reproduced later.

7. INTERNAL TENANT BENEFIT ENGINE
Compute eligible_realized_savings only when reliable inputs exist; for synthetic demos, use labelled simulated savings. Define approved sharing fraction p, pool = max(0, eligible_savings*p) with contractual/legal review. Allocate pool by supported method (e.g., eligible shop-time usage, not blindly total monthly load), cap every adjustment, apply largest-remainder or deterministic rounding such that sum allocations <= pool. Track common-area energy independently, tenant agreement period, and policy effective dates. No 'official VNM' column in internal-only mode. If contract or applicable local rule does not permit a scheme, disable issue/activate workflow; still allow hypothetical simulation labelled PROJECTION.

8. VNM RECONCILIATION (SEPARATE MODE)
Expected credit = applicable dated utility settlement rule(approved allocation, eligible settlement/export evidence, billing period, carry-forward). Compare expected with official household/shop bill credited amount. If allocation version, settlement, or matched period unavailable, result = UNVERIFIABLE, not zero difference. Do not calculate official credit using gross generation alone.

9. SOLARMATCH ALGORITHM
Input -> validate -> calculate roof-constrained/usage-informed feasible kWp range -> apply policy/connection suitability caveats -> HARD FILTER by service area, kWp service range, relevant credentials/evidence, project type, timeline when known -> weighted ranking over comparable documented fields -> return score breakdown + uncertainty. Example initial weights: technical 0.30, price 0.25, experience 0.20, O&M 0.15, verified reliability 0.10. Missing rating => missing/unknown, not zero-star fake. Referral/sponsored placements kept separate. If no qualified candidate, return none and unmet criteria. Quote values override indicative directory prices in later comparisons.

10. SMARTEXIT (RESIDENTIAL)
Determine actual legal ownership and outstanding obligations first. Case fully paid: may require share buyer/transfer but no funding shortfall; case financed: check contractual responsibility. CP-SAT integer/binary vars encode eligible replacement selection, divisible share units and optional additional contributions; enforce consent, budgets, transfer policy, funding, and approved-allocation workflow. Objective minimize financial disruption and preference mismatch. Proposal remains DRAFT until human consent and external approval.

11. HELIOS ARCHITECTURE
UI chat -> API identity/org scope -> Gemini with concise personality instructions + curated product/policy knowledge -> allowlisted function-call request -> backend rechecks scope -> deterministic service/database -> redact to minimal relevant result -> Gemini explanation with source/assumptions. Allowlisted read tools: get_my_statement, get_property_summary, compare_system_sizes, explain_benefit_allocation, find_installers, compare_quotes, get_project_status, get_help_article. No write tools in MVP; no raw SQL. Prompt injection from uploaded PDFs is untrusted content. When unknown, answer uncertainty; when asked for other tenant's figures, decline and offer aggregate public information if permitted. Keep Gemini API key server-only.
Helios voice: delightful and short, occasional sunshine pun, never mocking or flippant about money. Example: 'Let me shed some light ☀️ The fixed charge stayed ₹X, while energy charges fell ₹Y; here's the calculation.' Do not present model guesses as verified legal advice.

12. VISUAL MALL 3D DESIGN
GLB/glTF generic model stores geometry_key per shop. Shop state and metrics loaded from API, not material names. Colour legend green active, amber pending, blue proposal, grey other, red issue; badges provide noncolour meaning. Camera rotation, zoom, floor filtering, shop click; on low devices show accessible 2D floor grid with same information. Models lazy-loaded, compressed, no real mall trademark/location used without permission; present as generic synthetic demo.

13. SECURITY, FAILURE MODES AND SCALE
JWT/session bound to user; API resolves membership and role; policy engine checks org/property/project/tenancy. Storage private; metadata with source provenance. Async job idempotency for OCR/CSV reports; optimistic version checking for policies. Indexed org queries, pagination, cache non-sensitive aggregate metrics. Audit sensitive writes. On AI failure, regular dashboard and deterministic calculations remain available. On tariff-unknown, simulation marks assumptions or unavailable. On provider database-empty, display no verified match, no fabrication. On missing VNM settlement, unverifiable.

14. TEST STRATEGY

Integration: API role scope, quote ownership, tenant turnover, file isolation, versioned tariff updates, Helios tool scope and prompt-injection attempts.
E2E: three workspaces, synthetic mall 3D/2D, compare PV sizes, choose provider, installer quote, tenant statement, Helios explanation, residential regression.
Property tests: sum of tenant benefit allocations <= allowed pool; financial totals match components; zero export/generation edge case; cannot leak cross-org records.

15. ENVIRONMENT / DELIVERY
.env.example with GEMINI_API_KEY placeholder only; production secret manager; migration and seed command; dev startup guide; synthetic CSV schema, test fixture dates and source states. Git commit/PR per sprint, preserve deploy; production feature flags for unvalidated tenant billing and VNM arrangements. 8. DATA DICTIONARY (SELECTED)

active_organisation: UI context, never alone an access grant.

benefit_type: INTERNAL_ADJUSTMENT | APPROVED_VNM_CREDIT | PROJECTION | NONE.
source_state: SYNTHETIC | ESTIMATED | USER_CONFIRMED | PROVIDER_UPLOADED | OFFICIAL_APPROVED | UNVERIFIED.
meter_kind: MAIN_DISCOM | SUBMETER | GENERATION | EXPORT | SEPARATE_DISCOM.
period: start/end date/time with timezone and source granularity.
kwh: energy (NOT kW power; NOT currency credit).
capacity_kwp: PV nameplate power; not equivalent to monthly kWh generated.
benefit_policy_version: immutable formula/effective date/consent provenance.
allocation_approval_state: DRAFT | CONSENTED | SUBMITTED | OFFICIALLY_APPROVED | REJECTED.
installer_verification_status: VERIFIED_WITH_EVIDENCE | UNVERIFIED | EXPIRED.
financial_amount: integer paise or exact decimal rupees, never binary floating point.
